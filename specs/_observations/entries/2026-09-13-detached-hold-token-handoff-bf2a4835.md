- 2026-09-13 [planwright] A caller that takes a cross-process (detached) hold through a command substitution — ALLOC_LOCK_TOKEN=$("$LEDGER" lock "$UNIT") in allocation-adapt.sh and allocation-feedback.sh — has a window where the child has published the lock and the token has not yet reached the parent. A signal there leaves a detached hold whose token nobody holds, recoverable only by a token-less unlock. Closing it needs a handshake that survives the substitution (the caller nominating its own token, or the lock being taken on behalf of the parent pid the way orchestrate-lock does with --owner-pid), which is a lock-lib design question rather than a ledger one. Surfaced adopting the lock primitive (universal-binary Task 1).
