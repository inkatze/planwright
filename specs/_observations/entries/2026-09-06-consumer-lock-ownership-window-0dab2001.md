- 2026-09-06 [planwright] The acquire-window lock leak fixed in fleet-state.sh is still open in the seven scripts that take the fleet lock from outside. Each runs "fleet-state.sh lock", sets its own HOLD_LOCK after that call returns, and releases through "unlock"; a signal between the successful lock and the flag leaves the trap declining to release a lock the process holds, wedging fleet writers until the stale break. fleet-state.sh could close its own copy by gating release on the owner token, but a consumer cannot: unlock is unconditional and accepts no token, so the fix there requires the token to cross the process boundary, which is the deferral recorded as lock-family-token-crossing. Scripts: fleet-attention, fleet-throttle, fleet-audit, fleet-usage-gate, fleet-tower-marker, fleet-liveness, fleet-worktree-track.
