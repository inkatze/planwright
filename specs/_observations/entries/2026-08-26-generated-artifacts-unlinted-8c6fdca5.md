- 2026-08-26 [planwright] every local gate lints tracked files by glob (`lint:shell` over `scripts/*.sh`, `lint:yaml` over `config/ .github/`), so an artifact a script GENERATES is checked by nothing — a heredoc body is an opaque string to shellcheck, and an emitted workflow is not on yamllint's path. Two real defects reached inception task-2's sixth review round through that gap: a yamllint line-length violation in the workflow `inception-scaffold.sh` emits, and an unquoted `$HOME/...*` glob in the pre-commit hook it emits (shellcheck SC2231) under which a `$HOME` containing a space made planwright read as absent, every guard skip itself, and a staged credential commit. Both were caught by a reviewer rather than by the gate, and both are trivially tool-detectable once the generated file is on disk. Covered here by per-artifact assertions in the scaffold's own test suite; the general shape — emitters owe their output the same linters their source gets — is uncharacterized and worth a guard that finds emitters rather than one test remembering.
