- 2026-09-06 [repo] The echo-discipline sanitizer is defeated by the echo it feeds: sanitize_printable strips control bytes but leaves backslashes, and dash builtin echo re-expands \0nnn into the very control bytes just stripped. Reproduced through allocation-ledger append, whose refusal path printed a real ESC byte from a printable-only attempt argument. 157 echo+sanitize_printable call sites sit in /bin/sh scripts; the bash-shebang ones are unaffected because bash echo does not expand by default. The fix shape is printf %s at these sites, not a wider sanitizer.
