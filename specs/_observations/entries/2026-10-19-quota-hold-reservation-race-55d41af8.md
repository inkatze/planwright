- 2026-10-19 [planwright] scripts/quota-hold.sh moves the push reservation into place before it sets PUSH_TOKEN, so a TERM delivered between the two (the trap runs between commands) exits without releasing the reservation, and the next joiner of that hold will not push it. Seen as a CI flake in test-quota-hold killed-push once its wait polled faster (test-throughput Task 8); reproduced by widening the window with a sleep. Setting PUSH_DIR and PUSH_TOKEN before the mv is safe because release_reservation only removes a record carrying its own token.
