- 2026-07-26 [planwright] drain-gates.sh mangles non-ASCII gate text in its own report. The echo-safety strip (tr -d '\000-\037\177\200-\237' under LC_ALL=C, D-7 echo discipline) removes bytes 0x80-0x9F, which are exactly the UTF-8 continuation bytes of most punctuation: an em-dash (E2 80 94) or en-dash (E2 80 93) in a **Gate:** bullet emerges as a lone 0xE2, so the report line is invalid UTF-8 and the sentence is mangled. Two live instances as of 2026-07-26, both pre-existing: specs/observation-recording/tasks.md:211 (F1-F5) and specs/operator-dialogue/tasks.md:143. Downstream cost is more than cosmetic: grep over the captured report treats it as binary and silently matches nothing, so a report consumer cannot reliably search it. The strip is right to exist; it just needs to be codepoint-aware (strip C1 only when it is a real C1 codepoint, or restrict the byte strip to C0+DEL and handle C1 after decoding). Surfaced by the /self-review pass on planwright/chore/free-text-gates (PR #321), whose new gate parentheticals hit it and were reworded to ASCII as a workaround.
