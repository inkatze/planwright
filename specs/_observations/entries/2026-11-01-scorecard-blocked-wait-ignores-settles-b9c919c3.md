- 2026-11-01 [planwright] The tower scorecard's blocked-worker wait (scripts/tower-queue.sh report) runs a question from its born line to an acknowledged line and counts every other question open until the window end, so a question settled by evidence, or by a picklist window's default, keeps accruing wait after the worker stopped waiting. With picklist defaults now common, the measure overstates blocked time; ending a wait at the first settled or default window line for that item would fix it, and the committed fixtures would need their numbers recomputed.
