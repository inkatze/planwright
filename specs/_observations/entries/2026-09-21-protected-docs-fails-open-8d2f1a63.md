- 2026-09-21 [planwright] Overlay shadow protection fails open: 6 of 39 doctrine docs are protected, enumerated as a hardcoded string in scripts/resolve-rule-doc.sh and mirrored in docs/overlays.md and customization-overlay's D-11 and REQ-B1.7, so any doc not manually added to all three is silently shadowable by an overlay. The REQ-B1.7 test asserts each *named* doc resolves in core, which catches a protected doc being renamed or removed but cannot catch a doc that should be protected and never was: the list is validated against itself rather than against the doctrine tree. Surfaced by the point-of-use split scoped today, which moves the routing, prose-classification, presentation and audit-record half of finding-categorization.md into a new sibling finding-routing.md; the parent is protected, the child would not be, so a split undertaken purely for instruction-budget reasons would quietly widen what an overlay may override, including the prose-disposition rules once they land in the deferred half. Nothing in the split's own review would have flagged it; it was found only because the scoping worker enumerated what the change breaks. The durable fix is to invert the default, protecting every doctrine doc and enumerating the shadowable exceptions instead, so a new doc or either half of a split is protected on creation and the rarer, more deliberate act carries the enumeration burden. Cheaper interim guard worth having either way: fail when a doctrine doc appears in neither list, turning a silent gap into a loud one.
