- 2026-09-02 [planwright] Two gaps in the per-unit allocation lock, both spanning scripts/allocation-ledger.sh and its holders (allocation-adapt.sh, allocation-feedback.sh). First, PLANWRIGHT_ALLOC_LOCK_HELD suppresses the ledger's mid-append 'did I lose the lock' assertion as well as the nested acquire, because that assertion is gated on a token the held path leaves empty, and there is no public verb a holder can call to re-verify ownership; a hold broken by the stale sweep therefore appends with no check. Second, both holders acquire the lock before installing their release traps, so a signal in that window leaks the lock until the stale break, with the owner token lost.
