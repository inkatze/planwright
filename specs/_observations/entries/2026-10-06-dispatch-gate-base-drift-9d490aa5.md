A fetch by any other worktree in between can base a worker on content the gate never compared. Both the single-spec dispatch window and scripts/orchestrate-meta-step.sh compose it this way. Candidate fix: dispatch-fetch.sh prints the fetched SHA, the gate reads the brief at it, and fleet-dispatch-worktree.sh takes that SHA as --base, refusing one that is not origin/main.
