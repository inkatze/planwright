- 2026-09-03 [planwright] fleet-lifecycle-closure Task 4: fleet-streamjson.sh stop takes no election of its own, and release_locks deletes journal.lock, recover.lock and launch.lock on presence alone, without consulting the holder pid that lock_take itself honours. The process class does not cover the gap: launch, recover and answer are separate invocations, so none of them carries the _supervise argv or appears in the worker's pid files, and a stop whose process probe comes back empty walks straight on to the locks. Concretely, a stop concurrent with an in-flight launch (lock held, supervisor not yet spawned) reports the tree free, removes the launch's election lock, and deletes the .init staging file and the fifos it is about to hand the worker; the launch then comes up unreachable, and with the lock gone a third launch can win a second election - the exact double-supervisor outcome launch.lock exists to prevent. The same shape lets a stop void a live recover's election, and lets it break a journal.lock an answer is mid-rewrite under. Closing it needs a design call this task does not carry: whether stop takes each lock before releasing it (and what it reports when it cannot get one), or whether the lock class only releases locks whose recorded holder is provably gone. Task 5 (headless stop) and Task 6 (fleet-cleanup process) inherit the same shape by design, so the decision wants making once, in the bundle rather than per rung.
