- 2026-08-19 [planwright] A design decision may name a fix that ships "out of band as a chore PR" (D-11 in fleet-lifecycle-closure does exactly this for the WorktreeCreate hook contract). Nothing tracks whether that PR ever lands: the kickoff found the hook still broken on every installed machine, blocking all worktree creation and therefore all dispatch, with the bundle itself unable to fail on its absence because the fix sits outside its task graph. An out-of-band fix named in a design decision needs either a task, a gated deferral, or a dispatch-gate record; prose in a rejected-alternatives paragraph is not a ship mechanism.
