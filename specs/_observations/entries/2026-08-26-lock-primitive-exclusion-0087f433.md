- 2026-08-26 [planwright] The repo-wide advisory-lock shape (atomic mkdir to acquire, rmdir/rm -rf to release, spin with a stale break) does NOT reliably provide mutual exclusion on the current support bar. Measured with 12 concurrent same-unit writers doing acquire / read-modify-write / release: 13 interleaved critical sections over 10 rounds under the mkdir shape, 0 over the same 10 rounds under an atomic-symlink-create shape (ln -s to acquire, rm to release). It reproduces from 3 concurrent writers upward and under both dash and bash, so it is not shell-specific; a single fresh mkdir contest IS exclusive (1 winner of 12, every round), so the loss appears in the release-and-reacquire cycle rather than in mkdir itself. model-allocation Task 2 switched its own new per-unit allocation lock to the symlink shape with an OWNER TOKEN carried in the link target, which also closes the release-clobber window the mkdir family documents as a known limitation (a holder returning after its lock was broken deleting the current holder lock). scripts/fleet-state.sh and the rest of the lock family still use the mkdir shape and were deliberately left untouched: they are other specs contracts. Worth a spec-owner decision on whether the family should adopt the same primitive, and worth reproducing on a second machine before treating the measurement as universal. Reproduction harness shape is in the Task 2 PR discussion.
