- 2026-10-05 [planwright] A stream-json supervisor holds its journal lock across the fleet-attention.sh decide call, which blocks on the fleet-state lock. When the fleet lock's owner died (2026-10-05 14:48 to 15:03), every fleet-streamjson.sh answer and steer failed with "journal lock busy" for the full stale threshold, and workers sat on prompts nobody could answer. obs:d45387c2 (gap TWO) already records that a wedged attention shell-out becomes a journal outage because the journal lock refuses to break a live holder; this is the same window observed in production, reached through a dead fleet-lock owner rather than a hung attention store. The dead-owner break itself is universal-binary Task 1.3's (fleet-state onto lock-lib, which already probes owner liveness); this fragment records only the lock-scope part for that task to pick up: do not hold the journal lock across the attention call, so one stuck lock cannot cascade into the other. The wedge loses requests, not only delays them: while the fleet lock was dead-held, a worker's new control_request hit "journal lock busy", handle_line took the attention_failure path, which needs the same fleet lock and also failed, so the request was never journaled and pending never showed it; two workers sat about 98 minutes on a request nobody could see, recovered only by stop then recover (the resume re-sent it). The fix therefore also needs a retry or deferred journaling of the receipt, not only an early break of a dead-owner lock.
