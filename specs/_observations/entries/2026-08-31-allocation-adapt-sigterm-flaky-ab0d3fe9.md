- 2026-08-31 [planwright] test-allocation-adapt.sh case 13k is machine-speed flaky: after synchronizing on the lock symlink it sleeps a fixed 0.2s and then SIGTERMs the engine, but on a fast machine the resolve completes and exits 0 inside that window, the signal hits a dead pid, and the case fails spuriously (observed 4-of-5 locally; CI runner at PR #375 hit it once). The deterministic shape would block the engine inside the critical section (a PATH stub gating on a fifo) so the signal always lands while the lock is held.
