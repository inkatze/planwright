- 2026-10-25 [planwright] The write arms (own-branch and scratch) approve a target with a trailing slash whose last component does not exist yet, such as rm -rf <zone>/d/link/; a parallel tool call that creates that component as a symlink before the rm runs makes GNU rm follow it out of the zone. ob_mark orders writes within one command only.
