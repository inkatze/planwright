- 2026-09-02 [planwright] The config-mirroring guards in the allocation and fleet-allocate suites assert only one direction, that every key in a test fixture's embedded copy of config/defaults.yml exists in the shipped file. Nothing asserts the reverse, so a knob added to config/defaults.yml and absent from a suite's fixture is uncaught, and test-allocation-clamps.sh carries no such guard at all. The fixtures are also free to ship non-shipped VALUES with no signal, which is how a suite can silently test against a default the product does not have.
