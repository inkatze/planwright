- 2026-10-05 [fleet] A tower can't carry the operator's approval to its workers. Each worker's permission check rightly treats tower messages as peer input, so every off-script approval (for example, a workaround for a step that can't run) forces the operator to visit each worker's pane, which defeats the point of a tower. Decisions that should hold fleet-wide have to live in operator-owned config the workers already trust (the step catalog, planwright.local.yml) rather than in relayed messages. planwright should give operator-scoped approvals a home there, a verifiable channel that workers honour and peers can't forge.
