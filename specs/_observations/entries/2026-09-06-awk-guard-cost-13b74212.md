- 2026-09-06 [repo] check:echo-safety runs a character-at-a-time awk tokenizer over ~137 files and is now the most expensive non-test guard in the aggregate, roughly an order of magnitude per byte more than check-cdpath line-regex approach. Acceptable today and it buys precision a grep cannot have (command position, quote state, heredoc bodies, case patterns). Worth knowing before a second tokenizing guard is added over the same corpus, and worth noting that mawk is the fastest awk in common use; BSD awk and gawk are slower on this shape.
