- 2026-10-01 [planwright] Pausing tmux workers with SIGSTOP (2026-09-29) closed their windows: each pane ran fish -c wrapping the launch, the wrapper exited when its child stopped, and the claude workers were left stopped and orphaned (PPID 1, no terminal), so they could not be resumed and had to be killed and relaunched from on-disk state. The fleet has no sanctioned pause. Candidates: a fleet pause verb that stops dispatch and asks each worker to reach a safe checkpoint (commit WIP, park) instead of freezing processes; launch workers via exec so the pane process is the worker itself; and have the tmux backend keep the pane alive (remain-on-exit) so a stopped worker stays resumable.
