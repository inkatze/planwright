- 2026-09-02 [planwright] Operator request 2026-09-02: reshape /polish (and the gate-wiring contract it implements) on two fronts. FIRST, relocate sign-off to the merge per the signoff-belongs-at-merge decision: the checklist /polish and /self-review generate becomes a pre-MERGE checklist, not a pre-ready one, and their handoff prose must stop describing the ready flip as the approval. SECOND, the subject-line marker strategy is confusing and its safety is repo-configuration-dependent rather than designed. gate-wiring puts [pending-sign-off] at the end of the commit SUBJECT and the checklist regenerates by grepping subjects in base..head. Under squash merge the subjects become relic text in the squash body and the PR title (guarded marker-free by check-commit-msgs --marker title) becomes the mainline subject, so nothing leaks. inkatze/planwright is squash-only (allow_merge_commit=false, allow_rebase_merge=false) so this holds HERE, but it holds because of a GitHub repo setting an adopter may not share, not because the design prevents it. An adopter permitting merge commits gets every marked subject permanently on mainline, each claiming to be pending sign-off about work that was approved at merge; gate-wiring calls this "an accurate record", which is wrong once approved and cannot be corrected because history is never rewritten. Candidate strategy: move the signal off the subject and into a git TRAILER (e.g. Planwright-Sign-Off: PS-1), with checklist regeneration reading trailers rather than subjects. Trailers are conventional metadata, invisible in git log --oneline, do not corrupt the conventional-commit subject, survive squash into the body exactly as subjects do, and read as provenance rather than as a live status claim when they do land on mainline. This also removes the need for the --marker subject placement lint and the --marker title rejection, since a trailer can never become a squash subject.
