- 2026-09-02 [planwright] The shared fleet advisory lock (fleet-state.sh) is now taken once per worker dispatch, on top of fleet-attention, fleet-throttle, fleet-worktree-track, and the bound counter. Two pre-existing costs become reachable at that rate: try_acquire re-resolves the stale-lock threshold through config-get.sh on EVERY busy retry (about six forks per spin, up to 1000 spins), and the lock has no fairness, so an in-process spinner samples it far more often per unit of wall time than the out-of-process spinners in fleet-attention/fleet-throttle, which fork the whole script per attempt. Under a burst of dispatches those consumers can burn their retry budget and fail closed — fleet-attention returning 2 means an awaiting-input record is lost. Caching the threshold once per process is a small fix; fairness is a lock-family redesign.
