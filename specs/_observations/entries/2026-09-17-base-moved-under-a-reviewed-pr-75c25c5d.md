- 2026-09-17 [planwright] Merging one PR can invalidate the verification of every other open PR, and the ready-guard is what catches it. PR #472 was merged to main at 23:57. PR #471 had by then passed a self-review, a non-Anthropic panel pass and six Copilot rounds, with green CI on its own head, and PR #468 had passed its own checks earlier. Neither had merged main since. When the tower tried the draft-to-ready flip on #471 the ready-guard refused it: one commit behind base, so the branch had not been verified on a current head. That refusal was correct and worth more than the flip would have been -- every one of those review rounds ran against a base that no longer existed. The same single merge also left #468 blocked on a stale failing check, so one merge invalidated two independently reviewed branches at once. Two things follow for the tower. A convergence judgement should include a behind-base check, not only CI colour and thread counts, because a branch can be green and drained and still unverifiable. And when a PR merges while others are open, the reconcile sweep has an opportunity to notice the now-stale siblings and surface them, rather than each being discovered separately at its own flip or merge attempt.
