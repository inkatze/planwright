- 2026-11-19 [planwright] scripts/bounded-run.sh spends its -k grace as a count of 0.1 s polls, each reading the process table, so the grace outlasts its seconds wherever that read is slow (a loaded host with many processes took about 20 s of a 10 s grace); a caller racing it with its own timer (run-tests.sh's worker backup KILL) can end the runner before its KILL lands. A wall-clock bound on the grace would remove the dependency.
