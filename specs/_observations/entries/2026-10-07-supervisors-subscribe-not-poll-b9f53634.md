- 2026-10-07 [planwright] Towers and orchestrators should wait on workers through completion signals, not polling: a cross-session idle subscription (notify_when_idle, one-shot and re-armed after each notice), a worker briefed to message the tower when it finishes, halts or needs input, and the attention store written by worker hooks. Pane captures and status renders belong only in a long-interval failsafe (a dead window, a mid-turn stall, a permission prompt). Today the orchestrate --watch loop under tmux is described as a polling metronome. Observed 2026-10-07: subscriptions plus a ten-minute failsafe covered three tmux workers, with two wrinkles worth designing for: an idle subscription armed on an already-idle session fires at once with its last turn summary, and worker hooks do not rewrite the attention row when a cross-session message resumes a worker, so the row reads idle while it works.
