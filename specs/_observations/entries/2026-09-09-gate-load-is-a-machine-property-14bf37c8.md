- 2026-09-09 [planwright] The full gates load sensitivity is a property of the MACHINE during fleet operation, not of the gate alone, and every measurement of the false-red rate taken without recording that is measuring noise. Concretely: a batch of gate runs was killed by the OS for low memory, and a check at that moment found 132 claude processes live on the host. So mise run check is not merely parallel within itself, it competes with an active fleet of towers and workers. That is a far better account of why test-fleet-streamjson.sh c12 and c20 and test-fleet-throttle.shs re-engage assertion all fail under the full gate and pass in isolation than "flaky test" ever was, and it explains why c12 could not be reproduced earlier at a 3-second window: the window is not a constant, it is whatever the host leaves. METHOD CONTAMINATION WORTH ADMITTING: running gates back-to-back to measure flakiness ADDS the very pressure being measured, so a rate collected that way is biased upward by the collection. WHAT A FUTURE MEASUREMENT MUST RECORD alongside each pass/fail: concurrent claude process count, load average, and available memory at the moment the suite starts. Without those columns the earlier figure (13 of 43 runs, 10 unexplained) cannot be compared against anything later, because the denominator silently changed with how busy the box was. A gate that is green on an idle laptop and red during fleet operation is not intermittent; it is load-dependent, and the two need different remedies -- the first invites a retry, the second invites either fixing the race (as c12 was) or bounding the concurrency the suite runs under.
