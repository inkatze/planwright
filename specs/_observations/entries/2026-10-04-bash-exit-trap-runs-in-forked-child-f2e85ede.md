- 2026-10-04 [planwright] A bash suite with an EXIT trap also installs a TERM handler that runs that trap, and a background child forked from it ('sleep 5 &', '"$@" &') inherits both until it reaches exec. Signal that child in that window and the child runs the parent's EXIT trap: a probe on 2026-10-04 saw 285 of 300 immediate TERMs run it. Any suite whose EXIT trap is cleanup (trap 'rm -rf "$tmp"' EXIT, the house pattern) and that TERMs a background child it just started can delete its own sandbox mid-run. The tmux launch harness hit exactly this under gate load, its watchdog TERMing a just-forked timer, and now guards tlh_teardown to the process that set it up and kills such children with KILL. Worth a sweep of tests/ for EXIT-trap cleanups beside kill of a fresh background pid, or a shared guard in the suites' trap helpers.
