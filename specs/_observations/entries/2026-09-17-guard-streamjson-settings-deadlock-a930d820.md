- 2026-09-17 [planwright] fleet-dispatch-guard.sh check-launch and fleet-streamjson.sh launch disagree about where the worker-settings pin lives, and the default dispatch rung cannot satisfy both. Linting the launcher argv (fleet-dispatch-guard.sh check-launch <root>/scripts/fleet-streamjson.sh launch <worker> <scope> ...) is refused with risk 20: "no explicit non-auto permission-mode source in the argv - pass --permission-mode <mode> or --settings <worker-settings fragment>". Adding --settings is then refused by the launcher: "refusing --settings in the launch argv - the worker-settings pin is structural (fleet-autonomy D-19, REQ-E1.4)", and --permission-mode is refused the same way. The launcher does pin the fragment structurally (it appends --verbose --permission-prompt-tool stdio --settings <fragment> to the claude argv), so the guard precondition is met in substance and the refusal is a false positive on the wrong shape: check-launch is written for a claude command line, while the stream-json rung is dispatched through a planwright script. Worked around this step by linting the effective claude argv instead (exit 0). Either check-launch should recognise the launcher as a shape whose settings posture is structural, or the launcher should expose the effective argv for linting (a --print-launch).
