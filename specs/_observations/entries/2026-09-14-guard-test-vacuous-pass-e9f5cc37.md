- 2026-09-14 [planwright] A corpus-driven guard harness (tests/smoke-worker-dispatch.sh, 2026-09-14) shipped three ways to report PASS while testing nothing: an empty or tab-mangled corpus passes with zero rows (no row-count floor), every defer-expected row passes against a root with no guard in it (defer is indistinguishable from an unwired hook because decide() maps any non-allow output to defer), and in CI mode its deployed-vs-checkout comparison can never print anything but IDENTICAL. Every other guard in this repo fails closed on exactly these shapes. A corpus harness needs: a minimum row count, a known-allow canary asserted before the corpus runs, and validation that the expectation column is one of the legal values.
