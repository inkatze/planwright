- 2026-09-05 [planwright] allocation-apply.sh is now the advertised entry point for every launch surface, and it calls allocation-adapt.sh, which takes the per-unit allocation lock. That lock is NOT reentrant: allocation-ledger.sh honours PLANWRIGHT_ALLOC_LOCK_HELD only in its append arm, never in its lock arm. So any caller already holding a unit lock that invokes allocation-apply self-deadlocks and spins the full retry budget (~100s at 20ms with a sleep fork per spin) before failing closed. No current caller does this, and nothing documents the constraint. Worth either making the lock verb honour the suppression variable, or documenting never-call-under-the-lock on allocation-apply. Surfaced by the model-allocation Task 6 review; not fixed there because it is Task 2 locking territory.
