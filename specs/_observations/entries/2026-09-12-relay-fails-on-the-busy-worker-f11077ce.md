- 2026-09-12 [planwright] doctrine/inter-orchestrator-coordination.md opens its relay section with "A tower steers and observes a live, busy worker mid-task - not a paused one", and the mechanism it specifies does the opposite: buffer-paste submits ONLY into a fully idle worker, and a worker with any work in flight queues the paste unsubmitted. Measured across seven relays on 2026-09-12. Submitted: three relays into sessions sitting at an empty prompt with nothing running. Did not submit: a 42-line relay (the CLI collapsed it to a paste placeholder), and single-line relays into three workers that each had a background shell running - exactly the mid-task case the doctrine names as the target. The attribution header the primitive prepends also makes every relay at least two lines, so even the short form is never a bare one-liner. Two compounding consequences: the tower cannot steer a worker that is actually working, and a queued paste leaves the input box in a state no later paste can submit either, which the tower cannot clear because it may not send keys. The doctrine and the primitive are individually coherent and jointly wrong about when the channel works; whichever is corrected, the claim that buffer-paste serves the busy-worker case should not survive as written.
