- 2026-09-24 [planwright] scripts/resolve-steps.sh spawns about ninety processes per invocation because each of its reads (config-get --layers twice, resolve-catalog twice, resolve-dispatch-isolation) re-resolves the four overlay roots and re-runs its own grep and sed per layer; one resolution costs 0.15-0.5 s depending on load, and tests/test-resolve-steps.sh, at about 140 invocations, is the slowest file the suite carries (28 s at loadavg 2, 137 s at loadavg 29). A once-per-process overlay-root read shared by the readers, or a batched mode that returns several keys or both catalog views in one call, would cut the cost for every caller, and the runner that fires five points per unit inherits it as it stands.
