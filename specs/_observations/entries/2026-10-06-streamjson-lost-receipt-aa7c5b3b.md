- 2026-10-06 [planwright] A stream-json worker's permission request is lost, not only delayed, when the supervisor cannot journal its receipt: with the journal lock busy behind a wedged fleet lock, the control_request is never journaled, so `fleet-streamjson.sh status` keeps reporting `running`, `pending` lists nothing, and `answer` refuses the request as not journaled (exit 3). wpe-task-4 sat 54 minutes on such a request, and the only way out was stop, recover and steer. obs:1eb24a7e already records the lock-scope cause and that the receipt needs retried or deferred journaling; this adds what it does not: the receipt could be re-derived from events.jsonl once the lock frees, and `status` should flag a trailing unanswered control_request in events.jsonl, so the stall is visible without reading the pane.
