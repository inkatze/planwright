- 2026-09-14 [planwright] fleet-streamjson.sh close force-clears the journal lock after stopping only the tracked worker and supervisor pids, so a third-party journal writer (answer, steer, alarm-scan, or a writer still in its cleanup) can be holding it: close removes the link, that writer releases into a successor, and two writers interleave a read-modify-write of the journal. The behaviour predates the lock-primitive adoption - close always cleared unconditionally - so this is a shutdown-protocol question rather than a regression: close should refuse while a journal holder is live, or wait a bounded budget and then force. Raised by a Copilot review thread on the universal-binary Task 1 PR, and matching the fork already queued in that PR body.
