- 2026-09-17 [planwright] Copilot review latency on this repo is 4 to 5 minutes, not the 10 to 15 the tower brief assumes, and the difference changes the right poll strategy. Across six consecutive review rounds on PR #471 (submitted 23:26:31, 00:20:15, 00:41:45, 01:08:58, 01:30:39, 01:51:28) every requested review landed within about five minutes of the request. The worker was briefed to wait at least twenty minutes in-turn and to treat silence as unreviewed, which is the right posture for the failure case but means each round spent minutes in a 30-second poll loop after the answer had already arrived. A poll that starts at a short interval and backs off would return the same safety with less wall clock, and over six rounds the saving is real. The twenty-minute floor before declaring a review absent should stay: the cost of reading a silent bot as a pass is much higher than the cost of waiting. Only the polling cadence inside that window wants tightening.
