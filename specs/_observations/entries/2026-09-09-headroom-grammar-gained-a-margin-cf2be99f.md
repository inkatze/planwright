- 2026-09-09 [planwright] skill-drift(instruction-headroom): the declared-exception grammar gained a required margin field, so specs/instruction-headroom/design.md:271 now describes a form the guard no longer accepts. It reads `declared-exception|<surface>|<reason>` (reason mandatory); the shipped grammar is `declared-exception|<surface>|margin=<N>|<reason>` for every margin-bearing surface, with the `margin=` tag load-bearing rather than decorative: a bare positional number cannot be told apart from a reason that opens with digits and a pipe, so an author who forgot the margin had one borrowed from their own reason text. The tag reserves that prefix in exchange -- a reason whose first segment reads `margin=...` is refused as a malformed margin, while `use-site:` surfaces keep the three-field `declared-exception|<surface>|<reason>` form because they never reach the headroom check, where <margin> is the surface headroom margin at the moment the exception was granted. The behaviour it adds, and what D-11 should say: an exception defers a restoration but does not license further spending, so a run whose margin falls BELOW the declared one is a widening and a fail-closed ERROR, remedied by a compensating trim in the same change or by governed relief (raise|), never by editing the declared number upward. The margin is checked before and independently of the warning bands, because a surface can widen while sitting comfortably above target -- the silent stretch over which the shipped corpus actually drifted to its floors. Two interactions worth pinning in the spec rather than leaving to the implementation: an entry whose ratchet evaluated while its floor is INTACT is marked used, so the staleness sweep does not tell an operator to delete the one thing holding the surface in place; but past the floor it stays unmarked, preserving the D-11 rule that a declared-exception never silences a floor-breach and keeping the escalation line that explains why it went inert. A use-site surface never reaches the headroom check, so it carries no margin and one is refused; every other surface must carry one, and a margin-less entry is an error rather than an unratcheted exception. A margin naming a surface no run measures ratchets nothing, and is reported by the existing staleness sweep plus a line giving the reason, as a WARNING -- staleness is a cleanup warning by the grammar rule, and only the widening was made fail-closed. Landed in the guard and its tests; the spec is Ready, so folding this in is a /spec-draft amendment plus a kickoff re-anchor, not an edit from here.
