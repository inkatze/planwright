- 2026-10-06 [planwright] The mkdir-based advisory locks (scripts/orchestrate-lock.sh and its siblings) are not mutually exclusive on hosts whose mkdir is uutils coreutils (0.10.0, the rust-coreutils default on Ubuntu 26.04): two concurrent `mkdir <path>` calls both exit 0 in roughly a third of 200 trials on tmpfs, while the raw mkdir(2) syscall (python os.mkdir) never double-wins and GNU mkdir is still installed as gnumkdir. Two racing orchestrate-lock.sh acquires on one spec both returned 0, so two towers can both hold one per-spec lock. Seen while testing the meta step. Candidate fix: take the lock through a primitive that reports EEXIST faithfully (an mkdir(2) wrapper, or ln/flock), and have check-lock-primitive.sh race the primitive itself.
