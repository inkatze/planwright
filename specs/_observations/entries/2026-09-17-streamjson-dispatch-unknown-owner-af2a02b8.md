- 2026-09-17 [planwright] Every stream-json dispatch registers as unknown-owner, and the documented way to fix it is the one thing an operator must not do. fleet-streamjson.sh launch prints "fleet-register: no tower identity available (set $PLANWRIGHT_TOWER_ID, or pass --session-id/--pid); recording this dispatch as unknown-owner". Of the three remedies it names, launch accepts none: its usage has no --session-id or --pid, and it does not forward them to fleet-register. That leaves exporting PLANWRIGHT_TOWER_ID in the launching shell, which leaks into the worker environment and makes test-fleet-stuck-detector.sh fail with "env identity did not resolve through the surface" on any branch. So the registry cannot attribute a dispatch to the tower that made it on the shipped default rung. fleet-presence.sh publish takes --pid and works fine, so the identity exists in the session; it just has no path into the dispatch record. Fix shape: give launch a --session-id/--pid passthrough to fleet-register, or have it derive the owner from the live presence record instead of the environment.
