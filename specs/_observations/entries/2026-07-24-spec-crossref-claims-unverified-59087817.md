- 2026-07-24 [planwright] A spec bundle can assert cross-reference claims about itself ("D-1 through D-4 and D-9 cite REQ-A1.1", "every decision below cites it") that are simply false, and no shipped guard catches it: spec-validate, check-doc-links, check-ledger and markdownlint all passed over a bundle carrying three such false claims (found by hand during merge-currency-guard Task 1 self-review; D-3 and D-9 did not cite the REQ the prose said they did). check-doc-links validates doctrine cross-references, not intra-bundle REQ/D-ID citation claims. Since spec-format makes citations the traceability primitive "everything else leans on", a claim-vs-reality checker (parse "<record> cites <ID>" assertions, or at minimum verify that every ID named in a Citations: field exists and every record a Done-when names as cross-referencing actually contains the reference) would turn a class of silent doc rot into CI signal. Seed for a future spec; not acted on during the task.
