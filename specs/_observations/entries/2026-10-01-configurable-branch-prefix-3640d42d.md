- 2026-10-01 [planwright] Operator request 2026-10-01: make planwright branch naming flexible or customizable. Today the grammar is fixed: task branches planwright/<spec>/task-<ids> (bootstrap D-36) and flight branches planwright/flight/<flight-id> (tower-front-door D-11, chosen to keep existing parsers working).
