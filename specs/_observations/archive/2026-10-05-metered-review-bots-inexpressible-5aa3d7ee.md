- 2026-10-05 [planwright] Metered review bots cannot be expressed as steps. Usage and throttling (scripts/fleet-usage-gate.sh, scripts/fleet-throttle.sh, the fleet_usage_* knobs) read only the Claude account's signal, and a step's outcome set (custom-steps doctrine: passed, applied, halted, failed, skipped) has no quota-refused arm, so a bot triggered by a PR comment mention that answers with a quota refusal reads as an ordinary failure, and 'stop and never retry on quota' is not declarable. Also missing: 'full review on the first request, incremental after', and a post-pr step declaring that it moved the head so the bot is asked to review the new head. review-effectiveness REQ-E1.5 and REQ-E1.6 cover recording the bot's findings and naming the external angle, and custom-steps leaves plugin wrappers for external review tools out of scope; neither gives the step contract these arms. Wanted: on-failure outcomes that tell quota or refused apart from failed, a post-pr head-changed signal, and possibly a bot step kind carrying the full or incremental choice. Acceptance scenario is the one obs:6c3342a8 names. Likely home: the same custom-steps extension.
